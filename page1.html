Lamborghini – A Legacy of Excellence Founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini in Sant'Agata Bolognese, Italy, Automobili Lamborghini is an iconic Italian luxury sports car manufacturer. Renowned for its innovative design, exceptional performance, and powerful engines, Lamborghini represents the perfect combination of elegance, speed, and technology. Over the years, the brand has become a global symbol of luxury, exclusivity, and automotive excellence.
